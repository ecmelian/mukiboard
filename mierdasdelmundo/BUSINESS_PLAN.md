# mierdasdelmundo · Business plan

*Shits of the world. A game, a movement, a media network and a cleaning fund, in that order.*

Draft 0.1 · October 2026 · written for the founder, to be turned into a sponsor deck and a public manifesto.

## 1. The idea in one breath

mierdasdelmundo turns the shit on the sidewalks of big cities into a game (the app), the game into content (the channels), the content into money (sponsors, members, books, merch) and the money into clean streets (the cleaning fund). Every line feeds the next one. The app is the engine: every capture is a photo, a time, a place and a species that can be ranked, published, printed, sponsored and cleaned.

- **Mission:** make cities take shit seriously.
- **Vision:** the biggest open map of street shit in the world, and the crews that clean it.
- **Promise to supporters:** every euro is traceable to a street.

## 2. The movement

- **Name:** mierdasdelmundo, one word, lowercase, in every language.
- **Lines:** "Give a shit." / "La mierda importa." Sponsor format: "X gives a shit" / "A X le importa la mierda".
- **Tone:** blunt, funny, scientific when it has to be. We mock behavior, never people. No faces, no names, no doorsteps.
- **Look:** Helvetica, white and black, green only for the key things, 3D clay characters. Same as the app, same on stickers, books and thumbnails.
- **Languages:** Spanish and English first for content. The app already speaks six.
- **Who it is for:** dog owners who pick up, people who are tired of stepping in it, city nerds, journalists who love a ranking, and brands that want to show they give a shit.

## 3. The engine: the app and its data

What exists today: a phone-only web app, no store needed, in six languages, aware of 190 big cities. You photograph a poop, catch a species (32, five rarities, shinies), earn XP and badges, and the capture goes on a map with its time and place. Optional world sync shares sightings, a world map, a leaderboard and a city ranking.

What every capture produces: a photo, a timestamp, a location (rounded to about 11 m when shared), a city, a species and rarity, a trainer. In aggregate: hotspots, time-of-day patterns, a city ranking, streaks.

Next in the app, in this order:

1. Faces blurred on the phone before a photo is saved, plus a manual blur tool, plus automatic posting of captures to the official X account through a small reviewed queue.
2. A consent and licensing screen so captures can be used in videos, books and posts, with credit to the trainer.
3. "Adopt this shit" from the capture detail (see 4.4).
4. Monthly city challenges and seasonal leaderboards.
5. A "Shitty Index" per city, updated weekly from the data.

## 4. Business lines

### 4.1 The media network

Channels: YouTube (long episodes and Shorts), Instagram (Reels and carousels of the best captures), TikTok, X (the live feed of captures, faces blurred, posted by the app). Later a newsletter and a podcast.

| Show | Spanish title | What it is | Cadence | Pays for itself with |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| The Shittiest Cities in the World | Las ciudades más mierda del mundo | A ranking from app data, one city per episode, with an annual awards special | Monthly | Sponsors, tourism boards, press |
| Where Your Shit Goes | ¿A dónde va tu mierda? | The science series: how shit is made, where it goes after the flush, what happens when you can't shit, what happens when you shit where you shouldn't, the diseases it carries | Every two weeks | Health, water and pet sponsors, education licensing |
| The Shit Clock | El reloj de la mierda | How much of our lives we spend making, using and cleaning shit, one topic per episode (toilets, dog walks, sewers, diapers, cleaning crews) | Monthly | Sponsors |
| Pet Shit | Mierdas de mascota | Everything dog and cat: picking up, bags, the law city by city, vets, the best captures of the week | Weekly short | Pet brands |
| Shitty Behavior | Comportamientos de mierda | Street crews film shitty behavior (not picking up, dumping, littering, spitting), faces always blurred | Weekly | "X gives a shit" sponsorship |
| Cleanup Day | Día de limpieza | A crew cleans a hotspot, before and after, and who paid for it | Per cleanup | The cleanup's sponsor |

Formats that travel across all channels: the capture of the day, the species dex (one card per species), city rankings, "guess the city from the shit", before/after cleanups.

### 4.2 "X gives a shit": the sponsorship format

A brand sponsors an episode, a series, a cleanup or a whole city.

- **Package:** the naming ("Acme gives a shit" / "A Acme le importa la mierda"), the brand on the 3D badge of the episode, a mention in the video, the brand on the city page of the app, and a cleanup in their name with before/after content.
- **Tiers:** episode, month, city, year.
- **Rules:** sponsors never edit content and never choose the targets of Shitty Behavior. A sponsor funds cleanups, not opinions.
- **Good fits:** poop bags, pet food, vets and pet insurance, cleaning products, shoe brands, city tourism boards, water utilities, real estate, delivery companies.

### 4.3 Members and crowdfunding

- **Patreon (recurring):** Give a shit (name on the supporters wall, early videos). Big shit (a monthly print of the best capture, votes on the next city). Legendary (a cleanup credited to you every year, your capture in the book).
- **GoFundMe (one campaign per concrete goal):** "Clean one neighborhood for a month", "Print Book Vol. 1", "The first crew's equipment". Each campaign shows the same number: euros in, shits out.

### 4.4 Adopt a shit: individual sponsorships

- **Adopt a cleanup:** a street, a day, a crew. Your name on the cleanup video and on the map.
- **Own a poop:** a capture gets your name on the map and in the book, with a printable deed of ownership.
- **Own a shitty behavior:** an episode of Shitty Behavior "owned" by you ("This episode is brought to you by Marta, who gives a shit").
- **Mechanics:** payment links first, manual tagging in the database, the app shows "Adopted by …" on the capture. Automate later.

### 4.5 Books and print

- **Mierdas del mundo, Vol. 1:** a coffee table book of the best shits. The best photos, the rarest species, the shittiest cities, the data, the science and the people who clean. Annual. Hardcover, square, around 200 pages. Print-on-demand first so there is no stock, offset once a volume sells.
- **City editions:** Mierdas de Madrid, Mierdas de Barcelona, Mierdas de Ciudad de México.
- **Also:** a calendar (twelve shits), postcards, posters of the species dex.
- **Needs:** licensing from contributors (the consent screen in the app), a curator, a designer. Contributors get credit and a discount.

### 4.6 Merch

- **Stickers of the movement:** packs with the 3D poop, the species, the badges and the "Give a shit" line.
- **Badges:** enamel pins of the 18 app badges. You can only buy the pin of a badge you have unlocked; the app shows a code for it.
- **Bags and dispensers:** branded poop-bag dispensers that clip to a leash.
- **Apparel:** T-shirts and caps, black and white with green only where it matters. Print-on-demand.
- A fixed share of every merch sale goes to the cleaning fund and the share is printed on the product.

### 4.7 Data and city services (later)

- Hotspot reports for city councils, neighborhood associations and property managers: where, when, trends, before/after a campaign.
- Anonymized open data for researchers and journalists.
- Sponsored poop-bag stations placed at hotspots, with a QR code to the app. The brand pays for the station and the bags.

## 5. The cleaning fund and the cleaning service

- **What:** a privately funded service that cleans the hotspots the app finds, starting with one city.
- **Where the money comes from:** 100% of donations, plus a published share of sponsors, books and merch.
- **Transparency:** a monthly public ledger. Money in, money out, streets cleaned, shits removed (counted with the app). This is the single most important trust signal for members and donors.
- **How it grows:**
  1. Pay existing local cleaning companies per job. No payroll, no equipment.
  2. One crew of our own in one city: two people, a cargo e-bike, a pressure washer, bags. Every cleanup is filmed for Cleanup Day.
  3. One crew per city, funded by that city's sponsors and members.
- **Check first:** cleaning public space may need a permit or a coordination agreement with the municipality (water use, waste disposal). Start with the conversation, not with the pressure washer.

## 6. Shitty Behavior: rules of the game

1. Faces are blurred on the device before anything is saved, and again in the edit. Plates too.
2. We film behavior, not people. No names, no following, no doorsteps, no children, no confrontations.
3. Public space only, one presenter and one camera, and we stop when asked.
4. A lawyer signs off the format once per country. In Spain that means the right to one's own image (LO 1/1982) and the GDPR; every platform also has harassment rules that decide whether the series can be monetized.
5. A public takedown address. We remove on request, no questions.

## 7. More ideas (what else)

- **Golden Poop Awards / Premios Mierda de Oro:** the shittiest city, the cleanest city, the best capture of the year. One press event a year. Rankings get coverage.
- **City challenges:** "Clean Barcelona in March" with a brand prize and a special badge.
- **The Shitty Index:** one number per city, updated weekly, that the press can quote.
- **Newsletter, "La mierda del día":** the best capture, one data point, one video. Daily or weekly.
- **Podcast:** scientists, cleaners, vets, city officials, the people who pick up after everyone.
- **School kit** for Where Your Shit Goes: biology and civics in one box.
- **Trainers of the month:** local ambassadors per city, a pin, merch, a shout-out.
- **Corporate cleanup days:** team building that ends with a before/after video.
- **The heroes:** collaborations with municipal cleaning services and cleaners' unions. They are the stars of Cleanup Day.
- **Washable chalk stencils** to flag a hotspot ("mierdasdelmundo" and an arrow). No stickers on public property.
- **The 3D characters** as toys, keychains and plushies.
- **Dog events and pet fairs:** a booth, a capture contest, stickers.
- **A sponsor map** on the website: every cleanup a brand or a person paid for.

Rough startup budget: trademark and domains, legal setup, Book Vol. 1 design and proofs, video gear, the first cleanup pilot and a buffer. Order of magnitude: a few tens of thousands of euros, raised with GoFundMe #1 and the first sponsor. Running the app costs close to nothing today (static hosting, free database tier).

Monetization thresholds to plan around: YouTube's partner program requires 1,000 subscribers plus either 4,000 public watch hours in a year or 10 million Shorts views in 90 days (at the time of writing). X's API tiers decide how many posts a month the app can publish.

## 9. Structure, legal and trademarks

- **Two entities, eventually:** a nonprofit association or foundation for the cleaning fund (receives donations, publishes the ledger) and a company for sponsors, books, merch and channels, with a contractual share to the fund. One entity is fine to start. Ask a gestor or lawyer before the first sponsor contract.
- **Trademark:** register "mierdasdelmundo" (EU, classes for apps, media, print, merch, cleaning services) and the sponsor slogan. Secure the domain and the handles on every platform now.
- **Photo licensing:** the in-app consent screen gives the movement a license to use captures in content and print, with credit. Contributors can withdraw.
- **Privacy:** GPS rounded, faces blurred, no user accounts, deletion on request, and the data layout stays public in the README.
- **Platform rules:** the series live or die by the harassment policies of YouTube, TikTok and Instagram. Shitty Behavior is designed around them (section 6).
